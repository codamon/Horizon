The method was demonstrated on OpenAI's GPT-OSS 120B model compressed to 60B parameters and quantized to MXFP4 format. This technique challenges a fundamental assumption in ML deployment that full precision always yields better performance, opening new possibilities for efficient model compression. The ability to beat full-precision performance could significantly impact how organizations deploy LLMs with limited compute resources. QAH applies post-processing techniques to recover and enhance model accuracy after aggressive compression, specifically targeting reasoning and coding capabilities in structurally compressed models. The approach works with microscaling formats like MXFP4 that use block-wise scaling factors instead of per-tensor exponents.

Common approaches include Quantization-Aware Training (QAT) and Post-Training Quantization (PTQ), which help mitigate accuracy degradation from reduced bit-widths. LLM compression involves optimizing both architecture and parameters to create smaller, faster models that remain cost-efficient to deploy.
