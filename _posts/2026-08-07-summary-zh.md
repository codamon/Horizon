## [Cloudflare 推出无 Chromium 的 AI 代理优先浏览器 Kitesurf，基于 V8 隔离技术](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/06/cloudflare-introduces-kitesurf-an-agent-first-web-browser-that-runs-entirely-in-v8-isolates-on-cloudflare-workers/) ⭐️ 7.0/10
