## [Princeton-Ant Group-Stanford Researchers Launch AQuA Framework to Prevent AI Research Agents from Corrupting Their Own Evidence](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/01/aqua-a-two-part-agentic-framework-for-autonomous-factor-discovery/) ⭐️ 7.0/10
